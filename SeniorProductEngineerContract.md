## Job Description

A senior engineer who wants to lead engagements as a contractor: you run the plan, the agents and the client relationship for a project, part-time or full-time, by the hour.

You run several AI agents at once on real client projects. You turn tickets and chat into tasks an agent can execute, review every pull request and reply before the client sees it, fix what the agent gets wrong, teach it the client's codebase and conventions, and bring the ideas it cannot have. You write the client's daily update. You are accountable for the result, and you are in the client's channel under your own name. Your cockpit is our operator console: the queue of tasks and approvals across your agents, the session into each agent's machine, the work diary the client sees, and the daily report.

## You will

* Lead one or two engagements or one of our teams: diagnosis call, written plan, team of agents, delivery — as the team lead named on the team's ever.tech and Cloc Now profiles.
* Operate up to 8 agents; review everything that reaches the client.
* Make the architecture and scope calls; say no to bad ideas, including ours.
* Mentor the operators and interns on your engagements.
* Contribute to the agent catalogue: new agents for your specialization, evals, public runbooks.

## You have

* 6+ years shipping software, at least two as the person responsible for a product or a team.
* Deep in at least one of our stacks (TypeScript/NestJS/Next.js, Angular, Rust, React Native, Kubernetes) and able to review the others.
* A track record with coding agents at scale (many PRs a day, several agents in parallel) and opinions about where they break.
* Client-facing: you can run a call, write a plan a CFO can read, and deliver bad news early.

## What you get

Hourly or monthly pay (role-specific below), remote-first, a real seat at client calls, a machine budget, model budgets that are not a bottleneck, your own agents to run, and authorship: the agents you improve carry your name in their changelog.

Terms: contract, 20–40 h/week, remote any time zone with 4 h overlap with EET, hourly rate in line with our Lead package ($49/h), paid through Upwork or by direct invoice — your choice.

Stack: TypeScript end to end (NestJS, Next.js, Angular, React Native), Rust and SolidJS for our newer platforms, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes on our own cluster, GitHub, Linear/Plane, Slack/Telegram/Discord. Agents run on Claude Code / Agent SDK, Codex, OpenClaw and OpenHands, chosen per agent, each on its own isolated machine; you operate them from our operator console. Runtimes change, judgement does not.
