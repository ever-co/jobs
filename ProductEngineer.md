## Job Description

You own client outcomes with a small team of agents. You can build the feature yourself and you choose not to when an agent can — your hands are for the parts that need judgement.

You run several AI agents at once on real client projects. You turn tickets and chat into tasks an agent can execute, review every pull request and reply before the client sees it, fix what the agent gets wrong, teach it the client's codebase and conventions, and bring the ideas it cannot have. You write the client's daily update. You are accountable for the result, and you are in the client's channel under your own name. Your cockpit is our operator console: the queue of tasks and approvals across your agents, the session into each agent's machine, the work diary the client sees, and the daily report.

## You will

* Operate 4–8 agents across 1–3 client engagements from our operator console; own their quality end to end; get certified for each agent you operate.
* Brief, review, correct and teach agents; maintain their project memory and skills.
* Write the daily update and run the weekly demo for your clients.
* Design and build the parts agents should not (architecture, security-sensitive code, data migrations), and pair with clients' engineers.
* Improve the agents themselves: evals, runbooks, skills, model routing; ship agent releases.
* Grow into leading one of our teams (e.g. JS/TS Ninjas): the lead is accountable for a team's output.

## You have

* 3+ years building and shipping web products; strong TypeScript (NestJS/Next.js or similar); PostgreSQL; Git fluency; tests as a habit.
* Real experience running coding agents on non-trivial work, including the failures.
* Product sense: you ask "should we build this" before "how".
* Clear written English; comfortable in client channels and calls.
* Nice to have: Rust, Kubernetes, React Native, Angular, open-source maintainer experience, prior agency or consulting work.

## What you get

Hourly or monthly pay (role-specific below), remote-first, a real seat at client calls, a machine budget, model budgets that are not a bottleneck, your own agents to run, and authorship: the agents you improve carry your name in their changelog.

Terms: full-time, remote in EU time zones or Sofia, competitive salary + a share of the operated agent-hours your agents bill, hardware + model budget, 25 days leave, conference budget.

Stack: TypeScript end to end (NestJS, Next.js, Angular, React Native), Rust and SolidJS for our newer platforms, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes on our own cluster, GitHub, Linear/Plane, Slack/Telegram/Discord. Agents run on Claude Code / Agent SDK, Codex, OpenClaw and OpenHands, chosen per agent, each on its own isolated machine; you operate them from our operator console. Runtimes change, judgement does not.
